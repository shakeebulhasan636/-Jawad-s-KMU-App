# Jawad's KMU App — MBBS Study Companion

Jawad's KMU App is an all-in-one digital study companion built specifically for MBBS students at **Khyber Medical University (KMU)**. It bridges the gap between massive medical syllabi and high-yield exam preparation by combining interactive testing, topic-level sub-filtering, custom AI tutoring, note summarization, and performance tracking.

## 🎯 Problem & Solution

### **The Problem**
MBBS students at Khyber Medical University face an overwhelming volume of medical content structured across modular subjects. Standard question banks are generic, unorganized by specific organ systems, and lack regional curriculum alignment. Students struggle to test themselves on specific sub-topics (e.g., Renal System vs. General Physiology) and lack an instant, intelligent medical tutor tailored to their level.

### **The Solution**
**Jawad's KMU App** delivers a localized, intelligent study ecosystem:
1. Practice AI-generated MCQs filtered by **Subject** and specific **Topic / Theme**.
2. Summarize dense lecture notes into high-yield takeaways.
3. Chat in real-time with **MedTutor AI** for clear medical explanations.
4. Track quiz scores, accuracy percentages, and completion time via a visual Analytics Dashboard.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Topic-Filtered MCQ Practice Bank:** Practice customizable sets of 5, 10, 15, or 20 questions filtered by Subject (e.g., Physiology) and precise Topic/Theme (e.g., Renal System).
* **AI Medical Tutor:** Interactive chat assistant tailored for medical pedagogy—delivers structured comparisons, pathophysiology breakdowns, and clinical correlations.
* **AI Note Summarizer:** Summarizes complex medical passages, textbook sections, and lecture slides into digestible study notes.
* **My Notes Hub:** Allows students to save, manage, and review important notes and study materials in one place.
* **Performance Analytics:** Tracks total quizzes completed, average score percentages, top scores, average test times, and visual score trend lines over time.
